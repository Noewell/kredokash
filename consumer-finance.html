AFFORDABILITY & RESPONSIBLE BORROWING

Credit should support the next step, not create a worse problem.

Before a consumer facility can be considered, KredoKash reviews relevant information about the applicant’s ability to repay. Product design, term and collections approach must remain appropriate to the borrower’s circumstances and the applicable programme.

Purpose

Understand the individual need the financing is intended to support.

Affordability

Assess available income, obligations and ability to make scheduled repayments.

Transparency

Provide clear information about the facility, repayment and applicable terms.

Support

Encourage borrowers to contact KredoKash promptly if their circumstances change.
