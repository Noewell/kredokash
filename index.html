FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What financing products does KredoKash offer?+
KredoKash's core financing model includes B2B working capital, invoice finance, import finance and asset finance, with additional merchant, personal and student products being developed or offered subject to the applicable programme.
How do I start an application?+
Use the Apply button or contact KredoKash with the financing requirement, amount, timing and relevant supporting information.
What should I prepare before applying?+
Prepare a clear description of what you need to finance, the amount required, the timing and any relevant business or transaction documents.
How does invoice finance work?+
KredoKash verifies a qualifying commercial invoice and the underlying receivable, advances an agreed percentage and receives repayment when the underlying customer pays, subject to the agreed structure.
Can KredoKash finance imports?+
Yes. Qualifying import finance can cover approved supplier payments, deposits, shipping, customs, logistics and inventory acquisition, subject to transaction due diligence.
What documents will I need?+
Requirements depend on the product and transaction. Business and transaction verification can include financial information, invoices, purchase orders, contracts, supplier information, shipping documents and evidence supporting repayment capacity.
