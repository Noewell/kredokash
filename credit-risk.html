Credit & Risk

Disciplined credit. Continuous monitoring.

KredoKash assesses the evidence behind each request and keeps monitoring active after a facility has been approved and disbursed.

Start your application ↗

Credit begins with evidence, not just the amount requested.

The information considered depends on the product and transaction, but the assessment remains grounded in borrower, cash-flow, transaction and risk evidence.

CREDIT ASSESSMENT

Understand the borrower.

  • Identity and business verification
  • Financial information and cash flow
  • Trading history and existing obligations
  • Repayment capacity
TRANSACTION RISK

Understand the commercial activity.

  • Contract, purchase order and supplier
  • Customer or debtor and invoice
  • Delivery, shipping, customs and goods
  • Relevant security and controls
PORTFOLIO CONTROLS

Manage exposure across the portfolio.

  • Borrower, sector and product concentration
  • Tenor, liquidity and delinquency
  • Default and recovery performance
  • Use-of-funds discipline
ONGOING MONITORING

Approval is not the end of the credit process.

KredoKash monitors utilisation, repayments and changes in the borrower’s risk position. Portfolio-level monitoring helps inform future underwriting and risk decisions.

Performance

Repayment behaviour, delinquency and recovery performance are monitored over time.

Exposure

Concentration and outstanding exposure are considered as the portfolio develops.

Data

Transaction and repayment data progressively improve future credit decisions.

Control

Appropriate reporting, review and collection controls support responsible lending.
