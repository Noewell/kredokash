How It Works

A clear process from application to repayment.

KredoKash uses a documented credit process to connect assessment, approval, documentation, disbursement, monitoring and collection.

Start your application ↗

From application to renewal, every stage has a purpose.

The process applies the appropriate level of verification, assessment and monitoring to the product and transaction.

01

Apply

Submit a financing request and supporting information.

02

Verify

Verify the applicant, business, transaction and documents.

03

Assess

Review financial position, cash flow and repayment capacity.

04

Risk Review

Evaluate transaction, industry, security and concentration risk.

05

Approve

Move the facility through the applicable delegated authority.

06

Document

Complete financing agreements and security documentation.

07

Disburse

Release approved capital according to facility terms.

08

Monitor

Track utilisation, repayment and changes in risk.

09

Repay

Collect scheduled repayments through the agreed mechanism.

10

Close or Renew

Close after repayment or reassess the facility for renewal.

Approval is not the end of the credit process.

Once a facility is active, KredoKash continues to monitor performance and relevant changes in risk. This allows the credit process to remain connected to actual borrowing and repayment behaviour.

Information requirements vary. The documents and verification required will depend on the financing product, the borrower and the transaction being considered.

Structured credit for real business. Financing built around the transaction, the borrower and repayment capacity.
